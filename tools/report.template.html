What fell short of the GDD

  • No deploy. vercel.json is in the repo; connecting it needs a Vercel project and a GITHUB_TOKEN with read access to the shell repo.
  • No human playthroughs or videos. Feel tuning (takedown punch, slide-vault flow, man-to-mech camera) has not been judged by a person; there is no side-by-side capture of Aegis against AlphaMech. Values are the extracted baseline and unit-tested, not felt.
  • Target-machine performance unmeasured. Only the hardware-independent numbers above are real.
  • Component coverage. Chapter 5 was bot-verified with no components and with all three; the other six of the eight combinations were not run.
  • Marshal TTK was measured only with the bot: about 3.4 min (no components, 2 retries) and 3.1 min (all components, 1 retry) of game time with perfect aim. A human on Normal should be at or above 4 minutes, but that is unmeasured.
  • Runtime is likely short of 60 minutes. The bot clears each chapter in 3–8 sim minutes; a careful first-time player will take longer, but the areas are leaner than the GDD's per-chapter minutes.
  • Placeholder audio and stand-in art by design: synthesized SFX and score, subtitle-only VO, procedural hero assets. Files dropped at the manifest paths replace them without code changes.
  • Enemy navigation is steering plus stuck recovery, not a navmesh; enemies can snag on dense cover.
  • Gamepad is fully bound (menus and play) but untested on a physical controller.
